ABOUT POWERSMART

Helping Consumers Understand Energy Use

PowerSmart Australia is a data visualisation website created to explore appliance energy consumption in the Australian market. The website presents energy information through simple and easy-to-understand visualisations.

Our Purpose

The purpose of PowerSmart is to help consumers better understand the products available in the Australian market and how different product characteristics may relate to energy consumption.

The Television Energy Insights page focuses on screen technology, screen size, television brands, power consumption and energy star ratings.

About the Data

The television data used in this project comes from the Australian Government appliance energy rating dataset used in the COS30045 Data Visualisation exercises.

The dataset was explored and processed using KNIME before the results were presented using pie charts, bar charts and scatter plots.

About This Project

This website was developed as part of COS30045 Data Visualisation. The project focuses on communicating data insights to an audience through clear visualisations and supporting explanations.
